# Home and NA source search

Observed: 2026-09-05. Metadata-only feasibility check. No training, teacher edits, new split, image downloads, or new human labels.

**No verified source supplies teacher-compatible Home or NA labels.** The best narrow auxiliary lead is the IKEA US product release: its derivative category index contains **183 unique product IDs labelled `Throw pillow covers`**, plus **19 `Accent & throw pillows`**. These are existing seller category labels. They are not Usage labels, and neither count measures independent product families.

Teacher development context supplied by the existing audit: Home has one image/one family and is a `Cushion Covers` product. NA has 61 images/49 families across 14 types. The local audit explicitly distinguishes literal `NA` from one missing Usage label. See [existing data audit](../../data_intervention_20260905/REPORT.md). Therefore at-home outfits, pyjamas, missing metadata, unknown tags, and a generic household category cannot be directly relabelled Home/NA.

## Narrowed leads

### 1. IKEA US July 2025: useful Home-adjacent product-type source

[Jeffrey Zhou's original release](https://huggingface.co/datasets/jeffreyszhou/ikea-us-products-2025) contains 30,511 product records with `product_id`, `title`, `description`, `category_tree`, `style`, `image_urls`, `source_url`, `price`, `good_to_know`, `materials`, and `care_instructions`. It is a scrape of IKEA seller metadata with one hero image per product. `style` is explicitly an unused blank placeholder, not a Usage or NA label. The file listing reports 49,462,537 bytes for JSONL and 3,514,424,320 bytes for the image tar. Neither was downloaded. The source says research/noncommercial use and retains IKEA ownership of images/text; it does not establish unrestricted image redistribution rights.

[CommerceTXT](https://huggingface.co/datasets/tsazan/ikea-us-commercetxt) is a conversion of that same release, **not another independent dataset**. We downloaded two category indexes and two product metadata files per category. The [throw-cover index](https://huggingface.co/datasets/tsazan/ikea-us-commercetxt/blob/main/categories/throw-pillow-covers.txt) has 183 listed rows/183 unique SKUs; the [pillow index](https://huggingface.co/datasets/tsazan/ikea-us-commercetxt/blob/main/categories/accent-and-throw-pillows.txt) has 19/19. Saved samples include VILDPERSILJA covers 60582335 and 70582330: two colors within one named design. This shows why SKU counts cannot be called family counts. The conversion card declares CC0 but also research/noncommercial restrictions; do not read the badge as a fresh grant over IKEA images. The saved sample schema has an image URL, category, and seller description, but no occasion annotation. An attempt to query original rows via the HF filter service timed out after 30 seconds; exact counts above come from the derivative indexes.

Best next step, only if an auxiliary type task is wanted: inspect the original selected product records and their images, group named product variants, then check image hashes against all canonical teacher images without using protected labels. Keep source targets `Throw pillow covers` and `Accent & throw pillows`. This may help learn household textile shapes, but no evidence yet says it improves nine-class Usage. No image quality claim is made because images were not inspected.

### 2. IndoFashion: Ethnic-adjacent; gated and not direct occasion supervision

[Official documentation](https://indofashion.readthedocs.io/en/latest/tutorials/info.html) reports 91,166 train, 7,500 validation and 7,500 test images. JSON keys are `image_url`, `image_path`, `brand`, `product_title`, `class_label`. Its 15 documented categories are Saree, Women Kurta, Leggings & Salwar, Palazzo, Lehenga, Dupatta, Blouse, Gown, Dhoti Pants, Petticoats, Women Mojari, Men Kurta, Nehru Jacket, Sherwani, Men Mojari. Missing attributes are explicitly replaced with `NA`; this is **not teacher Usage NA**. Access requires a form and emailed script. No form was sent, no file obtained, and no reuse grant was verified.

[The authors' paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2021W/CVFAD/papers/Rajput_IndoFashion_Apparel_Classification_for_Indian_Ethnic_Clothes_CVPRW_2021_paper.pdf) describes ecommerce/search collection and class curation; it includes Myntra, so teacher-source independence cannot be assumed. Its class figure lists labels such as Western Top, Long Kurta, Dress, Patiala and Men Dhoti, differing from the live documentation. The released version would have to settle that discrepancy. Labels describe garment type, not the teacher's use occasion. Keep only as a later auxiliary source; it is not a no-access-barrier solution today.

## Other primary releases checked / excluded

- [IKEA-Dataset, Vassiliades and Bassiliades](https://github.com/valexande/IKEA-Dataset): more than 12,600 household-object images grouped by room, often with dimensions. Product/object unit; room/type metadata, no demonstrated Usage label. Large ZIP assets and no clear licence in the viewed root. No verified cushion-cover count. Less convenient than the newer SKU release; not evidence that all household images are Home.
- [DeepFurniture](https://www.kujiale.com/festatic/furnitureSetRetrieval): 24k scenes, 170k furniture instances and 20k identities, with category/style annotations. Generated interior/rendered furniture unit differs from fashion catalogue photos. Original release requires an agreement form; no form sent. No teacher Home/NA occasion taxonomy demonstrated. Third-party HF mirrors are not independent sources and cannot supersede the original terms.
- [ADE20K](https://github.com/CSAILVision/ADE20K): manually annotated scene objects/parts, rather than products with intended-use labels. Current release requires registration/approval. A cushion object or a home scene does not supply teacher Home. Excluded from direct Usage supervision; no assets fetched.
- [DeepFashion-MultiModal](https://github.com/yumingj/DeepFashion-MultiModal): person images with shape, fabric and color annotations. Its official README defines `NA` as the relevant part not being visible. This is a concrete false match, not support for teacher NA. Noncommercial research use with onward copying/distribution restrictions. Saved README supports the semantic exclusion.
- [Neural Loom, Indian Saree & Handloom Clothes](https://neural-loom.github.io/): authors report 580 normal-saree and 33 handloom originals captured with phones, then crops and augmentation. Existing collection labels concern textile type, not occasion. 26,100/1,185 augmented counts must not be called new independent examples. No clear reuse terms checked; download not attempted. Small auxiliary texture lead only.
- [Clothing Style Detection dataset, creator account](https://dev.to/thecodingdino/clothing-style-detection-recommendation-system-dataset-construction-3e6p): creator reports 6,864 manually labelled clothing instances over Comfort Wear, Street Wear, Office Wear, Party Wear, Ethnic Wear. Ethnic images came from the internet and other categories from DeepFashion. No accessible released annotation file or licence was verified. Comfort Wear is clothing comfort, not cushion-cover Home. Potential native Ethnic supervision, but currently unverified and dependent on source reuse rights.

## NA decision

The search found **no independent existing annotation policy equivalent to teacher Usage NA**. Its heterogeneous teacher support is not a visual class definition from which arbitrary external products can safely be assigned. Two tempting literal matches fail explicitly: IndoFashion NA means missing fields; DeepFashion-MultiModal NA means not-visible body/clothing parts. IKEA blank style also fails. Existing category labels such as wallet, perfume or shoe care could support separate auxiliary tasks, but selecting those types and calling every item NA would create new, unsupported labels.

No direct merge is recommended. Keep Home/NA scores honest and preserve both classes and the canonical split. This is a negative finding about the checked releases, not a claim that no suitable dataset exists anywhere. FashionStylist Home/Travel semantics are being checked in the companion search, not duplicated here.

## Bounded follow-up: original lineage and accessible Ethnic candidate

IKEA original revision: `4622140e11f51ebf0297e153d7639656a27a0acc`, from the saved [original release API](ikea_original_revision.json). Derivative revision: `1dc34c00e684512cdfa00b6650186dbd5081a704`, from the `X-Repo-Commit` response header in [derivative headers](ikea_derivative_revision.json). The original lists only README, image tar and JSONL; there is no separate LICENSE file in that listing. Its card metadata says MIT, while its prose retains IKEA ownership and research/noncommercial limits. We cannot establish that MIT grants unrestricted data/image rights, nor claim it definitively applies only to code. The derivative is explicitly a conversion of the original, but **183/19 remain derivative-index counts, not independently verified original counts**. Both original filtered/search-row requests timed out; fetching all 49.5 MB would exceed the assigned metadata limit. A subsequent pinned README request returned HTTP 429. Two exact image URLs already present in saved metadata were tried; each exceeded its 500 KB read cap, so neither image was saved or visually assessed. There is no screenshot and no isolated-product quality finding.

An accessible additional source is [Wardrobe Assistant, publisher Shahzaib Yaqoob](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shahzaibmalik44/wardrobe-assistant), version 1. Its public API download succeeded: **338,595-byte ZIP**, containing only one 2,823,943-byte CSV and **no bundled images**. We parsed all **5,443 rows**: `main_category` contains Men's Ethnic Wear **3,596**, Women's Ethnic Wear **974**, Indian Cloth **562**, Pakistani Cloth **311**. `occasion` is Wedding/Formal **4,169**, Party/Festive **598**, Casual **263**, Other **413**. There are **5,443 distinct product URLs and image URLs**, zero blank image URLs; these are not family or live-image counts. Counts and schema are in [wardrobe_counts.json](wardrobe_counts.json) and [followup_checks.json](followup_checks.json). The publisher says the records were curated from ecommerce platforms; source URLs in the sampled records point to Andaaz Fashion. The exact provenance of every normalized category/occasion/season field is undocumented, so label origin is existing publisher curation, potentially derived from seller metadata, not verified human occasion annotation. Its promotional image filename mentions Gemini; that does not prove the CSV is synthetic.

Wardrobe Assistant is a credible **metadata-backed auxiliary Ethnic-category candidate**, subject to verifying selected image-link availability, rights, family groups and teacher overlap. Its card says CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; linked seller images are not thereby shown to share that grant. `Men's/Women's Ethnic Wear` is a product category, not directly teacher Usage=Ethnic. Wedding/Formal and Party/Festive are fused source labels: do not split into new teacher labels automatically, and do not turn Other into NA. No human labelling is required to preserve these existing source targets. The ZIP, publisher description and count audit are saved locally; this follow-up has not acquired or inspected its clothing images.
